How the value is fitted

Trimmed least squares on the snapshot’s own tokened protocols. The refit runs on 102 of 113 peers — five dust listings and ten chain / centralised-exchange protocols are left out first, because their TVL is not comparable, and then the 11 worst-fitting peers are trimmed before the refit (see MODEL.md): log10(market cap) = 3.0161 + 0.5417 × log10(TVL) − 0.2248 × log10(chains), R² = 0.236 on all 113 peers (0.328 on the 102 kept). Two bounded adjustments follow: momentum × (1 + 0.25 × 7-day growth, clipped to −60%…+120%) and maturity × (0.9 + 0.2 × min(age, 1095d) / 1095d).

Held out, before it scored anything: leave-one-out median error ×2.844 (33% within 2×, n = 113); walk-forward median ×2.568 (fitted on protocols listed up to 2025-03-07, tested on the 25 listed after it; the 60/40 split gives ×2.525); 80% held-out interval ×10.115 around every value. Token values vary widely around TVL-implied levels — the interval says so instead of hiding it.

Coverage: 758 protocols — 198 launch candidates, 416 live tokens, 144 excluded (wrapped, staked, bridged, exchange or versioned products, carried at the $1 floor so the ranking stays a launch ranking; each still shows its model prediction). Watchlist: all 40 covered. Snapshot: 2026-11-14.

For the judge: the board ranks launch candidates by the fitted implied value, and live rows show the model’s fair value beside the market’s actual cap. Every row carries a costed seven-step farm plan. Nothing here predicts whether or when a token launches.
